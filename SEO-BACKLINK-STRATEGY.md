# Helios Digital Technology — Ethical Backlink Strategy

This is a planning document only. It does not authorize or perform outreach, account creation, submissions, paid placements, or link exchanges. Pursue only opportunities that are accurate, relevant, and editorially earned.

| Opportunity | Target website/type | Relevance | Suggested approach | Risk level | Suggested outreach angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Complete the company's own profiles | Verified LinkedIn and other company social profiles | Helps people and search engines confirm the company identity and discover its services. The live website currently links to some generic social homepages; profile ownership and exact URLs should be checked first. | Update company name, website, service descriptions, and contact details consistently. Link to the relevant service page only where the platform allows it. | Low | No outreach; keep first-party company profiles accurate. |
| Local business citations, if eligible | Reputable Ahmedabad/Gujarat business directories and an eligible Google Business Profile | Supports consistent business details for a company with a public location. | Confirm eligibility, ownership, address, and service-area details; submit only to a small number of legitimate directories that users actually consult. | Low to medium | Provide a concise, factual company description and verified contact details. |
| Relevant technology partner listings | Official vendor or partner directories, only where Helios has a current verifiable relationship | A true, current partner listing can help users find the company through a relevant technology ecosystem. | Verify partner status and directory rules before listing. Do not imply a certification or partnership that is not current. | Medium | Explain the service capability and the verified relationship; request an accurate listing. |
| Client-approved project stories | Client websites and relevant industry publications | Useful project write-ups can demonstrate work in context and send qualified referral traffic. | Publish only after client permission; describe scope and outcomes that the client can substantiate. Link naturally to the relevant service page. | Low to medium | Offer a short, factual joint story about the problem, solution, and lessons learned. |
| Practical engineering resources | Developer communities, technical newsletters, and reputable industry publications | Useful original guidance related to web development, application development, and AI-assisted development can earn citations from readers. | Publish genuinely useful, technically reviewed material; cite sources and avoid promotional filler. | Low | Offer an expert contribution or tutorial that answers a real audience question. |
| Original research or reusable tools | Relevant software-development communities and publications | A useful, documented tool or evidence-based study can attract natural references. | Build only when the company can maintain it; document method, limitations, and authorship. Do not invent data. | Low to medium | Share the resource with editors or communities whose audience would benefit. |
| Local professional participation | Ahmedabad technology groups, startup communities, and event organizers | Relevant talks and community work can generate real relationships and occasional editorial links. | Participate for the community value first; ask for a link only when a relevant event or member profile naturally includes one. | Low | Offer a practical talk or workshop on a topic within the team's real experience. |

## Avoid

- Link farms, private blog networks, mass directory submissions, automated comments, and bulk guest-post pitches.
- Paying for links whose purpose is to manipulate rankings, or exchanging links at scale.
- Fake reviews, fake profiles, fabricated client stories, and unverified awards or certifications.
- Outreach or profile submissions without a human review of identity, claims, destination, and privacy implications.

## Review before acting

Confirm that any named profile, partner relationship, client permission, business listing eligibility, project result, or public claim is current and authorized. No recipients or target sites have been contacted as part of this work.
